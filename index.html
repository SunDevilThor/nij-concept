The artist

About Jess

Jess is a tattoo artist working across Arizona, and her work leans delicate: thin lines, soft shading, and florals that sit on the skin like they grew there. Her four core styles are fineline, botanical, realism, and micro realism, and she also takes cover-ups and PMU brow appointments.

Every piece starts as a conversation. Bring the idea, the placement, and a reference or two, and the design gets drawn for you, not picked off a wall.

She books a limited seasonal schedule while school is in session, so the calendar moves a season at a time. Send your idea and a few dates, and she will find the window.
